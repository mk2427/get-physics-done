### L20: No Iteration Cap — Converge on Quality, Not Budget [review, scaffolding, workflow]
**Date**: 2026-04-16
**Trigger**: Iter-4 of scaffolding review returned REVISE on all 5 sections per the Rule-14 cap rule; orchestrator prepared to escalate on COUNT rather than on any genuine ambiguity. User correction: remove the iter cap entirely. Goal is solid results, not finishing within a budget.
**Rule**: Adversarial review loops have NO fixed iteration cap. The loop iterates until 0/0/0/0 — for as many rounds as it takes. "Iter-N reached" is never a reason to stop or escalate. Escalate to user ONLY on GENUINE ambiguity: a physics convention choice, a scope question, a MUST/SHOULD contradiction between governing documents, or a Fixer↔Critic disagreement neither can resolve from source material. Count-based escalation wastes user attention on issues the loop is designed to handle autonomously. This rule applies to CLAUDE.md Rule 14, AGENTS.md §7c, CLAUDE.md §4a Layer 2, and every future adversarial loop in this project.
